# Haven Lofts home page

Scope: the whole one-page site (`src/App.tsx` and its sections). Mode: Persuade. Visitor feels the space first, then books with Sara or Leah (call or text, via the booking drawer).
Keep: the script logo, the real salon photos, "Sara" spelling, the side-by-side Sara & Leah comparison idea. Remove: every drawn decoration (ivy, pendants, candles, neon script, slat drawing). No mustard or orange colour blocks (owners: too loud).
Suites: Leah's is the gallery wall over the mustard velvet sofa; Sara's is the quiet white room with the big window.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Haven Lofts styled after the salon itself, mostly the lounge, the coffee bar and the black mirror wall by the door: calm warm-white walls, one deep green feature wall, black and brass. Real photos are set like album sleeves. It refuses the loud colour-block look the owners rejected and the darkened-photo "BOOK NOW" salon template.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white #F3EEE6 walls and linen #E7DFD1 carry most of the page; the lounge green #3E4A37 and the mirror-wall black #1D1B18 fill whole sections; brass #B08A50 (sconces, mirror, planter) is the one accent, on buttons, the record label and bullets; cognac leather #8A4A26 for prices and links; Gloock display, Albert Sans text. Shapes: sleeve squares with 28px corners and a warm-white rim, pills.

STORY: The visitor sees the real lounge, reads that two owner-stylists work one-on-one in private suites in Lakeville, walks the rooms, scans services, compares Sara and Leah, and books with one of them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The lounge-green field under a solid page-colour nav. Desktop: "Haven Lofts" in Gloock at about 9rem, two lines, warm white; one sentence; "Book with Sara" (brass) and "Book with Leah" (warm white) on the left. The right half is the real lounge photo, large, in a sleeve with a warm-white rim. Phones: the photo comes first, then the name and the pills.

FORM: Living Room Sleeve, candidate 3 of 7 on the grounded list, seed:3d1a612fe477d879. Restyled after the owner pointed at the real lounge and mirror wall, then cleaned with the taste skill: no record, no stripes, no blur or resting shadows. Signature motion: the hero lounge photo settles in on load. Reduced motion: it simply appears.
