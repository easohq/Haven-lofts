# Haven Lofts home page

Scope: the whole one-page site (`src/App.tsx` and its sections). Mode: Persuade. Visitor feels the space first, then books with Sara or Leah (call or text, via the booking drawer).
Keep: the script logo, the real salon photos, "Sara" spelling, the side-by-side Sara & Leah comparison idea. Remove: every drawn decoration (ivy, pendants, candles, neon script, slat drawing).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Haven Lofts as its own 1970s living room, built from the salon's real materials in big flat fields: mustard velvet, walnut, olive wall, cognac leather. Photos are set like album sleeves. It refuses the cream-and-serif salon template and the darkened-photo "BOOK NOW" hero.

OWN-WORLD: Mustard #D4A62A owns at least a third of every screen; olive #4B5236, walnut #5E3A20 and cognac #B4522A fill whole sections; cream #F4E8CC only as rims and as text on dark fields; espresso #24180F ink. Gloock (soft, heavy 70s serif) for display, Albert Sans for text, nothing else. Shapes: sleeve squares with 28px corners, pills, one full circle (the record). The signature stripe: three stacked bands, cognac, walnut, olive, used once per major section, never as a border.

STORY: The visitor sees the real lounge, reads that two owner-stylists work one-on-one in private suites in Lakeville, walks the rooms, scans services, compares Sara and Leah, and books with one of them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mustard field under a mustard nav. Left: "Haven Lofts" in Gloock at about 9rem, two lines, espresso; one sentence; two pills, "Book with Sara" (espresso) and "Book with Leah" (olive). Right: the lounge photo as a 4:5 sleeve with a cream rim, a black record with a mustard label sliding out of its right side. The three stripes run behind the sleeve across the lower third, edge to edge.

FORM: Living Room Sleeve, candidate 3 of 7 on the grounded list, seed:3d1a612fe477d879. Signature interaction: the record slides out of the hero sleeve on load and turns slowly; each room sleeve's record peeks out on hover. Reduced motion: still, record half out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
